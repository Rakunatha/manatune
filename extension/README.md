# Manatune Chrome extension

Download this folder from your Manatune account (Chrome extension page) so it carries your site address, then in Chrome open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select the folder.

What it does: a capture record saves the page address, title, author and licence tags it can find (missing ones are recorded as UNKNOWN), plus a SHA-256 fingerprint of your selected text or image computed in your browser. The text or image itself is never uploaded. A repost saves a link with a short preview and your note; nothing is copied or hosted.

A capture record is evidence only. It is not proof of authorship, copyright ownership or registration, and it is not legal advice.

Permissions: activeTab, contextMenus, storage, scripting, and your Manatune address.
